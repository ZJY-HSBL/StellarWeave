# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-15

Initial reproducible research prototype.

- Synthetic stellar-field generator with known positions and injected flux.
- SciPy matched Gaussian smoothing/peak finding; optional Photutils DAOStarFinder.
- One-to-one matching and synthetic precision/recall/F1/position error/flux-binned completeness.
- FITS science-image quicklook and MAST public-image download helper.
- PNG/CSV/JSON outputs; automatic smoke-test CI.
- Versioned source ZIP and SHA-256 checksum published through GitHub Releases.

**Not included:** trained neural detectors, HST-aligned catalog evaluation, PSF flux fitting, uncertainty calibration, confirmed astronomical results.
